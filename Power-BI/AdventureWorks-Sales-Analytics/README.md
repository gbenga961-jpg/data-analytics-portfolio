# AdventureWorks Enterprise Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 1. Project Overview

The AdventureWorks Enterprise Sales Analytics Dashboard is a multi-page Business Intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI Desktop.

The project analyses enterprise sales data across products, customers, sales channels, and geographical markets. It transforms structured business data into interactive visual reports, helping stakeholders monitor performance, identify business trends, and make informed decisions.

The dashboard consists of three analytical pages:

- Executive Sales Overview
- Product & Customer Insights
- Regional & Operations Performance

## 2. Business Problems Addressed

Businesses need reliable and accessible information to understand sales performance, identify profitable products, evaluate customer activity, and monitor regional operations.

This project addresses the following business challenges:

- Limited visibility into overall sales and profitability.
- Difficulty identifying high-performing products and categories.
- Limited understanding of sales-channel contributions.
- Challenges comparing performance across geographical markets.
- Difficulty monitoring monthly sales and profit trends.
- Disconnected information across multiple business datasets.

## 3. Solutions Delivered

| Business Challenge | Analytical Solution |
|---|---|
| Limited visibility into business performance | Developed an executive dashboard presenting key performance indicators, including total sales, profit, orders, quantity sold, and profit margin. |
| Difficulty identifying profitable products | Analysed product-category revenue and profit contributions, identifying Bikes as the leading category. |
| Limited understanding of sales channels | Compared Internet and Reseller sales to evaluate their contributions to total revenue. |
| Difficulty identifying top-performing products | Created product-level analysis and a Top 10 products visualisation. |
| Limited regional performance visibility | Developed geographical comparisons to examine sales performance across countries and regions. |
| Difficulty monitoring business trends | Incorporated monthly and month-over-month performance analysis. |
| Disconnected business datasets | Structured eight core tables into a relational Power BI data model for integrated reporting. |

## 4. Dashboard Pages & Key Findings

### Page 1: Executive Sales Overview
![Executive Sales Overview](Adventuresale%201.png)

Provides an executive-level summary of enterprise sales performance.

**Key Performance Indicators:**
- Total Sales: $109.81M
- Total Orders: 121K
- Total Quantity Sold: 275K units
- Total Profit: $12.55M
- Profit Margin: 11.43%

**Key Findings:**
- Bikes generated the largest share of revenue and profit, contributing approximately $95M in sales and $11M in profit.
- Internet sales contributed $80.45M (73.26%) of total sales.
- Reseller sales contributed $29.26M (26.74%).
- Monthly profit analysis highlights changes in profitability over time.
- Geographical analysis compares key international markets, including the US, Canada, Australia, UK, France, and Germany.

## 5. Data Model

The Power BI data model contains eight core tables:

1. Customer
2. Date
3. Measure
4. Product
5. Reseller
6. Sales
7. SalesOrder
8. SalesTerritory

The model brings together information required for product, customer, sales, and geographical analysis.
